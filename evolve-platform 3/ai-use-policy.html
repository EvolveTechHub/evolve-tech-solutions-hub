AI Workspace Use Policy

Effective for the Preview release of Evolve AI Enterprise. This policy explains the responsible-use conditions for AI task processing. It supplements, and does not replace, the Evolve Privacy Notice.

Preview capability notice: the current AI task runner processes text instructions only. It does not inspect uploaded files, access your business systems, run code, change infrastructure or deploy software.

1. Eligibility and account use

  • You must use your own Client Account and keep your sign-in details private.
  • If you are under 18, use the AI workspace only with permission and appropriate guidance from a parent or legal guardian.
  • Do not share your account or attempt to access another client's tasks or results.

2. Information you may submit

  • Submit only information you are authorised to share and that is necessary for the task.
  • Do not submit passwords, API keys, access tokens, payment-card details, government identifiers, confidential personal records or sensitive personal information.
  • Do not submit confidential company information unless your organisation has authorised its use in this service.
  • File uploads and connected-system access are not enabled in this release.

3. Permitted use

  • Use the service for lawful, constructive business analysis, planning, learning and technology work.
  • Cybersecurity requests must concern systems you own or are explicitly authorised to protect. Requests for unauthorised access, credential theft, evasion, persistence or harmful activity are not permitted.
  • Do not use the service to make high-impact decisions about people without appropriate human review and independent validation.

4. AI-generated results

  • AI outputs can be incomplete, inaccurate, outdated or unsuitable for your circumstances.
  • Review calculations, assumptions, recommendations, code and technical proposals before relying on them.
  • Do not treat an AI response as legal, medical, financial, security or other professional certification.
  • Evolve does not claim that an AI response is a completed real-world action unless a separately confirmed tool or service has actually performed it.

5. Processing and saved task history

When you run a task, your text instructions and generated response are processed by the configured AI service and saved to your signed-in Client Account so you can review recent results. The Preview release applies an initial limit of three AI tasks per account in a rolling 24-hour period. Do not submit information you are not comfortable processing under the Evolve Privacy Notice and the configured provider's terms.

6. Service limits and changes

AI features may be unavailable, limited, changed or paused during Preview. Evolve may decline requests that are unsafe, unauthorised, outside the available capabilities or require additional safeguards. Human review is required before operational deployment or consequential decisions.

7. Contact

For questions about an AI task or data-handling request, contact Evolve through the contact details on the main website.
