Privacy and data protection

Privacy Notice

Last updated: 24 November 2026

This notice explains how Evolve Technology Solutions Hub handles information submitted through its website enquiry, quote-request and learner-interest forms. It should be read before submitting information.

Important: This is the website's initial privacy notice. Evolve should confirm its full legal business identity, contact address, retention schedule and applicable registration obligations with a qualified privacy professional before public launch.

1. Information we collect

Depending on the form you use, we may collect your name, email address, organisation (if provided), area of interest, requested service, budget currency and the message or project details you choose to submit. Please do not submit passwords, financial account credentials, government identification numbers or sensitive personal information through these forms.

2. Why we use the information

We use submitted information to respond to enquiries, review quote requests, follow up on training or learning interest, communicate about relevant services and maintain basic records of requests received.

3. How information is stored

When the submission service is configured, form submissions are stored in a Cloudflare D1 database connected to the Evolve website. Access to stored records should be restricted to authorised personnel. The website does not currently create learner passwords or full learner accounts through the registration-interest form.

4. Service providers and sharing

Website infrastructure and database hosting are provided through Cloudflare. Information may be processed by the hosting provider to operate the website and submission service. Evolve should not sell submitted contact information. Information may be disclosed where required by applicable law or to protect the website and its users.

5. Retention and security

Information should be kept only for as long as necessary to handle the request, maintain appropriate business records and meet applicable legal obligations. Evolve must set and apply a documented retention schedule. Reasonable technical and organisational safeguards should be used; no internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

6. Your choices and requests

You may contact Evolve to ask about the personal information you submitted, request correction, or ask for deletion where applicable law allows. We may need to verify the request before acting on it.

7. Children and young people

Where a learner is under the age at which they can independently provide valid consent under applicable law, Evolve should obtain any required parent or guardian involvement before collecting or using their personal information for a programme. Do not submit another person's information without appropriate permission.

8. Changes to this notice

This notice may be updated as the website and services change. The latest version will be published on this page with its updated date.

9. Contact

For privacy questions or requests relating to information submitted through this website, contact evolvetechhub.sol@gmail.com.
