Data and files


The Data Analytics workspace accepts CSV and .xlsx Excel workbooks for in-memory descriptive analysis, category comparison charts, AI interpretation of aggregate statistics and an experimental three-period baseline forecast when at least 24 consecutive daily or monthly observations are available, with consistent date precision and complete metric values. Forecasts use six rolling validation periods to compare a linear trend against a last-period baseline; the trend is selected only when it improves validation error by at least 10%. Indicative ranges are not formal prediction intervals or guarantees. Uploaded source files are not saved or sent to the AI model. Excel worksheets are converted in your browser; only the selected worksheet data is sent to the analysis endpoint. Use sample or de-identified data only.
